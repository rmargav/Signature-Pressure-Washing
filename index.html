COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our exterior cleaning services? We have put together answers to the most common questions we receive from property owners across Ottawa.

Still Have Questions?
What areas of Ottawa do you serve?
+
We proudly serve the Greater Ottawa Area and surrounding communities, including Kanata, Nepean, Orléans, Barrhaven, and more. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, just give us a quick call!
Are your cleaning methods safe for my property?
+
Yes, absolutely. We use industry-standard equipment and adjust our water pressure based on the surface we are cleaning. We also use eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are tough on grime but completely safe for your siding, landscaping, and pets.
Do I need to be home while you clean?
+
Not at all! As long as we have clear access to the areas being washed and a working outdoor water connection, we can complete the job while you are out. We will simply notify you when the work is done.
Do you handle both residential and commercial properties?
+
Yes, we specialize in both. From cozy residential driveways and roofs to large commercial storefronts, dumpster pads, and fleet washing, our team has the equipment and experience to handle jobs of any size.
How do I get a quote for my project?
+
Getting a quote is fast and easy. You can skip the endless online forms and call us directly at (613) 619-0916 for an immediate, over-the-phone estimate tailored to your property's specific needs.
